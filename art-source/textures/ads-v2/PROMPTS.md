# Facade panel ads v2

Generated with Codex's built-in GPT image generation tool on 2026-07-14.
The original PNGs in this directory are the generated source files; runtime
WebPs are exported to `public/assets/textures/ads-v2/`.

## Shared creative brief

- Use case: ads-marketing
- Asset type: emissive facade-panel texture for a cyberpunk city video game
- Style: premium original fictional branding, crisp silhouettes readable from
  a distance, sophisticated retro-futurist graphic design
- Lighting: controlled neon accents on a near-black opaque background
- Constraints: low average luminance; no pure-white background; no large bright
  rectangles; no transparency; no border, frame, mockup, watermark, real brand,
  extra text, or illegible pseudo-writing
- Runtime intent: bloom should accent the design rather than illuminate the
  whole rectangular plane
